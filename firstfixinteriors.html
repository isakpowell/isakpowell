First Fix Interiors

Branding and web design for a Sydney plasterer.

First Fix Interiors is a decorative plastering business based in Hurlstone Park, Sydney, operating for over 25 years. From Federation restorations and heritage-listed properties to contemporary new builds, they take pride in delivering exceptional work, but had no digital presence to show for it. I designed and built their brand from the ground up: logo, colour system, typography, business cards, and a full website. The brief was to make them look like the premium, experienced tradesperson they are: someone customers could trust before the first call.

brand built from scratch — logo, site, cards, system

A trade with no brand. A craftsman with no website.

Alan Peltier has been plastering Sydney homes for over 25 years. His work speaks for itself on any job site — but online, he didn't exist. No website, no brand, no way for potential clients to find him or understand what he was capable of.

The challenge was to build a brand that felt premium and trustworthy without feeling corporate. Tradespeople are judged immediately on how professional they look — the logo on the van, the business card handed over on site, the website that comes up when someone Googles them after a recommendation. Every touchpoint needed to carry the same weight.

Artistry In Plaster

The website leads with a bold, dark hero — dramatic photography, large type, and the tagline "Artistry In Plaster." It positions First Fix as craft-first, not just a quote-and-go tradie.

Services pages covering heritage restoration, decorative plaster, and new builds
Portfolio with before/after project photography and case studies
Private quote request form — name, project type, message
About page with 25+ years experience stats and location
Mobile-first design, fully responsive
Deployed on Netlify at firstfixinteriors.com.au
"Tradespeople who take pride in the work."

A brand system, not just a logo

The brand is built around two colours: a bold primary orange (#FE7700) and near-black (#000000) — with DM Sans as the typeface throughout. The combination reads as confident and modern without losing the grounded feel of a trade business.

The logo uses a house icon with a trowel motif, immediately communicating plastering and home without spelling it out. It works at favicon size, on a business card, and as a full header lockup — designed to scale from digital to print from the start.
